From completed appraisals to decision-ready intelligence.

ScoReview ingests appraisal PDFs, extracts structured data, and produces six-dimension alignment reports. Map KPIs to objectives, track cascade, and generate decision-ready analytics.

📄

Batch PDF Ingestion

Upload appraisal PDFs in batches. Structured data is extracted automatically — employee, KPIs, ratings, evidence, and manager comments.

🔬

Six-Dimension Analysis

Post-appraisal analysis across six dimensions: rating distribution, self-manager gaps, evidence quality, KPI achievement, outlier detection, and trend analysis.

📊

Rating Health Checks

Detect leniency, severity, central tendency, and clustering. Flag departments or managers whose distributions warrant calibration.

🗺

KPI-to-Objective Mapping

AI-assisted mapping of KPIs to organizational objectives, with deterministic validation. See what's aligned and what's drifting.

📈

Cascade Visualization

🔧

Corrective Action Tracking

Triggered decision rules generate corrective actions — calibration sessions, target recalibration, training plans — and track them to resolution.

The six dimensions of post-appraisal analysis

Every completed cycle is analyzed across six dimensions to reveal what the ratings actually say.

📊

1. Rating Distribution

Overall distribution across rating levels, compared against expected benchmarks. Spot skews before they become decisions.

⚖

2. Self-Manager Gap

Systematic differences between self-ratings and manager ratings. Large gaps signal calibration issues or misaligned expectations.

📝

3. Evidence Quality

Evidence notes scored for specificity and boilerplate. Weak evidence undermines defensibility — we quantify it.

🎯

4. KPI Achievement

Achievement rates by KPI, department, and perspective. Identify consistent misses and exceptional performers.

🔍

5. Outlier Detection

Statistical outlier detection at the manager and department level. Flag anomalies for human review.

📈

6. Trend Analysis

Multi-cycle trends in ratings, achievement, and alignment. See whether performance improves, stagnates, or declines.

From PDF to insight in four steps.

1

Upload Appraisals

Batch-upload appraisal PDFs. The system parses each document and extracts structured data — employee, KPIs, ratings, evidence, comments.

2

Validate Extraction

Extracted fields are validated against expected schemas. Unclear values are flagged for review — never silently guessed.

3

Analyze & Map

Six-dimension analysis runs automatically. KPIs are mapped to objectives with AI assistance and deterministic validation.

4

Report & Act

Decision-ready reports and dashboards are generated. Triggered decision rules create corrective actions with owners and deadlines.

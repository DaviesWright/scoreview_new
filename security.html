Security

How we protect your performance data — infrastructure, encryption, access control, and AI governance.

Infrastructure

ScoreView is built entirely on Cloudflare's global infrastructure. This means:

  • Global edge network — your data is processed at Cloudflare's edge locations in 300+ cities worldwide, reducing latency and improving performance.
  • DDoS protection — built-in Cloudflare DDoS mitigation protects against volumetric and application-layer attacks.
  • Web Application Firewall — Cloudflare's WAF inspects all incoming traffic for malicious patterns.
  • TLS 1.3 encryption — all data in transit is encrypted with the latest TLS standard.
  • Zero cold starts — Workers-based architecture eliminates cold-start latency and reduces attack surface.

Data Storage

Structured data is stored in Cloudflare D1 (SQLite at the edge). Documents and files are stored in Cloudflare R2 (S3-compatible object storage). Both are encrypted at rest.

Asynchronous processing (appraisal ingestion, report generation) uses Cloudflare Queues and Workflows, ensuring reliable, retry-safe operations without data loss.

Access Control

  • Role-based access control (RBAC) — users are assigned roles with specific permissions. Moderation panel access is restricted to authorized reviewers.
  • Authentication — all platform access requires authentication. API access uses scoped tokens.
  • Session management — sessions are managed securely with HTTP-only cookies and CSRF protection.
  • Audit trail — every action (who proposed, who reviewed, who decided, and when) is logged and immutable.

AI Governance

Our AI governance principles are security measures, not just ethical commitments:

  • AI proposes, people decide. AI never makes autonomous decisions. Every significant recommendation requires human approval.
  • Never manufacture missing evidence. The system never fabricates or infers missing data. Missing values are flagged for human review.
  • Separate evidence from inference. Performance data and competency assessments are measured independently. Potential is never inferred from performance alone.
  • Explainable outputs. Every AI recommendation includes the reasoning and evidence behind it. No black-box decisions.
  • Full audit trail. Every AI proposal, human decision, and system action is logged for review and compliance.

Data Privacy

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your performance data belongs to your organization. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Responsible Disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to security@bscscoreview.com. We acknowledge all reports within 48 hours and work with researchers to resolve issues responsibly.

Compliance

ScoreView is designed to support compliance with common data protection regulations including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar frameworks. Our audit trail and data governance features support your compliance obligations.

Questions?

For security-related questions, contact us at security@bscscoreview.com or through our contact page.

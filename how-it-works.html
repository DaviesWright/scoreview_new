Trustworthy Performance Data → Better Decisions

The full cycle, step by step

1

KPI Design & Submission

Managers design KPIs using the scorecard builder. Each KPI includes a formula, data source, target, weight, and time frame. Competencies are defined separately.

2

Automated Validation

The system scores each KPI against the 16-point compliance checklist and SMART rubric. AI proposes scores and identifies weaknesses. Nothing is changed without approval.

3

Moderation & Certification

Authorized reviewers work through the moderation queue. They accept, override, or request revisions. Certified scorecards lock in with a full audit trail.

4

Performance Cycle

Employees and managers work through the cycle. Evidence is collected. Ratings are assigned. The 9-box talent grid is populated from measured performance and assessed potential.

5

Appraisal Ingestion

Completed appraisals are uploaded as PDFs. ScoReview extracts structured data — employee, KPIs, ratings, evidence, comments — and validates the extraction.

6

Six-Dimension Analysis

The system runs rating distribution, self-manager gap, evidence quality, KPI achievement, outlier detection, and trend analysis. Results are compiled into a report.

7

Strategic Alignment

KPIs are mapped to organizational objectives. Cascade is visualized from company to department to individual. Alignment gaps are identified.

8

Decision & Action

Triggered decision rules generate corrective actions — calibration sessions, target recalibration, training plans. Actions have owners, deadlines, and tracking.

Governance principles

These principles are not limitations. They are the reason the data is trustworthy.

🛡

AI proposes. Evidence informs. People decide.

Every AI output is advisory. Every significant recommendation is explainable. Authorized humans review, override, and document — with a full audit trail.

📝

Never manufacture missing evidence.

If a value is missing, the system says so. If a KPI fails validation, it routes to human review — not to a fabricated guess.

🔐

Separate evidence from inference.

Performance data and competency assessments are measured independently. Potential is never inferred from performance alone.

📋

Full audit trail.

Every action — who proposed, who reviewed, who decided, and why — is recorded. Decisions are defensible.

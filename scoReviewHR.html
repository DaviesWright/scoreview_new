Trustworthy measures, before the cycle starts.

ScoReviewHR is the pre-appraisal assurance layer. It scores every KPI against a rigorous compliance checklist, validates targets and evidence, and routes anything questionable to human review — never to a guess.

📏

✅

SMART Validation

KPI Validation: the 16-point checklist

Each KPI is scored on 16 criteria across four dimensions. The result is a health score, a compliance verdict, and a clear path to fix what's weak.

🎯

Clarity & Specificity

Is the KPI specific? Does it describe exactly what is being measured? Is the wording unambiguous?

📊

Measurability

Can the KPI be measured with available data? Is there a clear formula or calculation method?

🏁

Outcome Orientation

Does the KPI measure an outcome rather than an activity? Does it link to business results?

🔗

Data Source & Formula

Is the data source identified? Is the formula clear and reproducible? Are targets and weights defined?

⏱

Time-Bound

Is there a clear time frame? Is the target realistic given the cycle duration?

⚖

Weight & Balance

Are weights assigned and balanced across perspectives? Does the KPI set cover the role's key accountabilities?

Human oversight, built in.

AI identifies issues. Evidence informs decisions. Authorized people decide — and every decision is documented.

1

KPI Submission

Managers submit KPIs through the scorecard builder. The system scores each KPI against the 16-point checklist and SMART rubric.

2

AI Flagging

KPIs that fail validation, lack evidence, or show boilerplate language are flagged. AI proposes fixes; nothing is changed without approval.

3

Human Review

Authorized reviewers work through the moderation queue. They can accept, override, or request revisions — with reasons recorded.

4

Certification

Certified scorecards lock in. The full audit trail — who proposed, who reviewed, who decided, and why — is preserved.

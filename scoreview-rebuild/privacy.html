Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

ScoreView ("we", "us", "our") is a performance intelligence and governance platform operated by PeopleOptix and Senrab ConsulTech. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our website and platform.

Data We Collect

Website Visitors

When you visit our website, we may collect:

  • Contact information you provide through forms (name, email, organization)
  • Anonymous analytics data (pages visited, browser type, approximate location)
  • Cookies for session management and preference storage

Platform Users

When you use the ScoreView platform, we process:

  • Employee performance data (KPIs, ratings, evidence, comments)
  • Organizational structure data (departments, reporting lines)
  • Appraisal documents (uploaded PDFs and extracted data)
  • User account information (name, email, role, permissions)
  • Audit trail data (who proposed, reviewed, and decided what, and when)

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the ScoreView platform
  • To validate KPIs, analyze appraisals, and generate reports
  • To respond to demo requests and inquiries
  • To improve our platform and develop new features
  • To maintain a complete audit trail for governance and compliance

Data Governance

Our governance principles apply to data handling as well:

  • AI proposes. People decide. AI never makes autonomous decisions about your data.
  • Never manufacture missing evidence. We do not fabricate or infer missing performance data.
  • Full audit trail. Every action on your data is logged and attributable.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Cloudflare's global infrastructure, including D1 (structured data), R2 (documents and files), and Workers (processing). Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. Access is controlled through role-based permissions and authentication.

For detailed security information, see our Security page.

Data Retention

We retain platform data for the duration of your subscription and for a reasonable period thereafter to support audit and compliance requirements. You may request data export or deletion at any time.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to certain processing activities
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@bscscoreview.com.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for session management and optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our website. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare — infrastructure, CDN, and security
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Google Fonts — web font delivery

Each service has its own privacy policy governing how they process data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via email or in-platform notification.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at hello@bscscoreview.com or through our contact page.

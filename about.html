About ScoreView

Performance intelligence you can trust — built by PeopleOptix and Senrab ConsulTech.

Our Mission

ScoreView exists to make performance data trustworthy. Many organizations have a performance-management process, but a process doesn't guarantee the resulting data is reliable enough to support people and business decisions. We close that gap.

Our platform validates measures before the cycle begins, reviews appraisal integrity after the cycle ends, and turns the resulting data into actionable workforce intelligence — all with human oversight built in.

Who We Are

ScoreView is a partnership between PeopleOptix and Senrab ConsulTech. Together, we combine deep HR consulting expertise with cloud-native engineering to deliver a platform that is both rigorous and practical.

We've seen the problems firsthand: poorly defined KPIs, inconsistent ratings, weak evidence, and strategic disconnect. ScoreView is our answer — a structured, AI-assisted, human-governed approach to performance assurance.

What We Believe

  • AI proposes. Evidence informs. People decide. Every AI output is advisory. Every significant recommendation is explainable.
  • Never manufacture missing evidence. If a value is missing, the system says so. If a KPI fails validation, it routes to human review.
  • Separate evidence from inference. Performance data and competency assessments are measured independently. Potential is never inferred from performance alone.
  • Governance is a feature. We don't hide human oversight — we make it a selling point.

How We're Built

ScoreView is a Cloudflare-native platform. Our infrastructure runs on Cloudflare Workers, Pages, D1, R2, and Queues — providing global edge performance, built-in security, and zero cold-start latency. The marketing site you're reading now is served from Cloudflare's global CDN.
